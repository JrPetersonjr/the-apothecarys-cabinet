Folk Herbalism, Lunar Cycles & Botanical Magic

At The Apothecary's Cabinet, every tincture, tonic, and balm begins in the rich soil of the Blue Ridge Mountains. We harvest herbs in harmony with the lunar rhythms and botanical seasons to capture maximum medicinal potency.

No synthetic preservatives, artificial fragrances, or filler oils ever enter our bottles. Only pure mountain spring water, raw unpasteurized honey, and organic cane alcohol are used as our extraction mediums.
